NEO-M9N SPECIFICATIONS (from u-blox datasheet):

Hardware:
  • Power: 3.3V VCC and I/O
  • Current: 25 mA typical, 67 mA max
  • Antenna: Ceramic patch (integrated)
  • Backup Battery: For hot start (charges when powered)

UART:
  • Default Baud: 38400 bps (8N1)
  • Protocol: NMEA 0183 (ASCII)
  • Update Rate: 1 Hz default, 25 Hz max

GNSS:
  • Constellations: GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, BeiDou
  • Channels: 92 tracking
  • Accuracy: 2.0m horizontal (multi-GNSS)
  • Cold Start: 24-29 seconds
  • Hot Start: 1-2 seconds
